Quilter Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Quilter is a local Markdown editor. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or
share personal information, document contents, usage analytics, advertising
identifiers, or diagnostics.

Documents and editor preferences are stored on this Mac. When you choose a
document or Library folder, macOS grants Quilter access to that location and
Quilter remembers the permission with a security-scoped bookmark. Quilter does
not upload the selected files. Recent documents and editor preferences are
stored locally using macOS preferences.

The preview runs from resources bundled with Quilter. When you activate a web
link in a document, Quilter hands that link to your default browser; the
browser's own privacy practices then apply.

Quilter does not use tracking, advertising, accounts, or third-party analytics.
For privacy questions, contact the developer through the support address
published with the app's App Store listing.
